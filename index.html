THE QUICK VERSION

How to play

01

Pick your angle

Move your mouse or finger to aim. On a keyboard, use the left and right arrows.

02

Make a match

Tap the board or press Space to fire. Connect three or more bubbles of the same color to pop them.

03

Clear the sky

Pop bubbles that hold others up and they all drop. Reach the clear target before your shots run out.

04

Make it count

Swap your next bubble when the color doesn't fit. Bigger matches and chain drops earn more points.

✹

Special visitors

Bombs pop nearby bubbles, rainbows match any color, line clears sweep a row, frozen bubbles take two hits, and stones only fall when unsupported.
